A correct computational result can reach an AI interpreter without the evidence that licenses its scientific use. We propose Guard Lab, an executable laboratory that separately evaluates support for a conclusion under received information and completion of the original research task. Exact population estimands and observationally equivalent potential-outcome populations make both point answers and justified non-answers inspectable. An executed workflow experiment uses two capable model configurations, generated arithmetic programs, numerical peer review, record selection and optional source retrieval. Its 144 receiver episodes compare a shared-transcript researcher, complete delegated records, analyst-selected records and three deliberate-loss policies. Both configurations preserved the design record in every natural handoff and retrieved it for every causal question after deliberate removal. All final scientific answers were warranted; scoped verification supplied no observed advantage. Ten literal citation-ID mismatches remain separately reported with a disclosed post-run audit. The development program will extend the mathematical structures and research environments to identify which information and verification policies preserve useful, warranted inference across AI research production.
